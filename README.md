# MAXOUT

Build a premium, mobile-first app for my clothing and fitness brand, MAXOUT.

Website: https://www.maxoutshop.com

The app should combine a full ecommerce experience with fitness tracking, meal logging, community challenges, exclusive drops, and member rewards.

The overall goal is to make MAXOUT feel like more than a clothing brand. It should feel like a complete fitness and lifestyle platform that users want to open every day.

BRAND AND DESIGN

Create a cool, premium, dark, minimalistic design.

Visual direction:

Near-black backgrounds

Dark charcoal cards and sections

Crisp white typography

Soft gray secondary text

Minimal use of accent colors

Large editorial product images

Smooth page transitions and subtle animations

Premium athletic and streetwear aesthetic

Clean spacing

Rounded cards and buttons

Strong typography

Mobile app-style bottom navigation

Responsive desktop version

Avoid bright neon gamer styling

Avoid overly complicated layouts

Avoid anything that looks generic or obviously AI-generated

The experience should feel like a mix of a premium athletic brand, a modern fitness app, and a luxury streetwear store.

Use the MAXOUT branding, logos, product photography, product descriptions, and website copy from maxoutshop.com wherever possible.
